## Full description (4000 chars max)

Can't stop hitting snooze? Puzzle Alarm makes you prove you're awake.

It works like the clock app you know: set a time, pick the days, add a label. The difference is
what happens when it rings. To turn it off you have to solve a puzzle, and the puzzle is new every
morning, written for you by AI. By the time you've worked it out, you're awake.

HOW IT WORKS
• The alarm rings full screen, even over the lock screen, until you solve the puzzle
• No auto-silence, and the Back button won't get you out
• One snooze per alarm, and you choose its length (1 to 30 minutes) or turn it off
• After the snooze, the only way out is the puzzle

PUZZLES THAT CHANGE EVERY DAY
• Mental arithmetic, number sequences, riddles, word unscrambles, word problems and odd-one-out
• Easy, Medium or Hard, and you can require up to 3 puzzles in a row
• A hint after two wrong answers, or ask for a different puzzle

BRING YOUR OWN AI
• Works with Claude (Anthropic), OpenRouter, OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible server
• Paste your own API key; the app picks the provider automatically
• No key or no internet? A puzzle made on the phone is used instead, so you're never stuck

RELIABLE
• Alarms survive restarts and time-zone changes
• Repeat on chosen weekdays or ring once

Your API key stays on your phone and is sent only to the AI provider you picked. No account, no
ads, no tracking.
