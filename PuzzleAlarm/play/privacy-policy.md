# Puzzle Alarm privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

Puzzle Alarm is published by Ishtah Software Solutions India Pvt Ltd ("we"). This policy explains
what the app does with your information.

## What the app stores

Everything stays on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- Your alarms (times, repeat days, labels, snooze settings)
- Your settings (AI provider, model, difficulty, number of puzzles)
- The AI API key you enter, if any
- The last few puzzle questions, so you don't get the same one twice

We don't run any server for this app, and we never receive this information.

## What the app sends, and to whom

When an alarm rings, and when you tap "Try a puzzle", the app asks the AI provider you chose
(Anthropic, OpenRouter, OpenAI, or a server you configured) for a puzzle. That request contains:

- your API key, so the provider can authorise the request
- the chosen difficulty and a short list of recent puzzle questions to avoid

It contains no name, contact details, location, alarms or device identifiers. The provider's own
privacy policy covers how it handles these requests:

- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy
- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

If you don't enter a key, the app makes no network requests at all.

## What the app does not do

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data.

## Permissions

- **Alarms and reminders / exact alarms**: to ring at the exact time you set
- **Notifications and full-screen notifications**: to show the ringing alarm over the lock screen
- **Run at startup**: to restore your alarms after the phone restarts
- **Vibrate, keep awake, foreground service**: to keep ringing until you solve the puzzle
- **Internet**: only to fetch puzzles from the AI provider you chose

## Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, and everything above is removed from your phone.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: write to the support email shown on the app's Play Store page.
